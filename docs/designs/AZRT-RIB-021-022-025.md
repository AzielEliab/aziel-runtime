## RIB-021 — counterparty receipts

Source: @MaracotResearch.

Policy is the open or policy receipt sealed before the exec. The exec receipt seals the act. `POST /v1/session/{id}/counterparty` then seals a counterparty statement bound to that act hash. A third party recomputes the commitment from the session chain. They do not need a copy that exists only on the client.

The caller cannot seal `verified`. A self-named issuer (the runtime, the client, the session, or the author) is refused and nothing is written. A missing counterparty row stays `absent`. A row whose commitment, attachment, or policy link does not recompute stays `unverified` and fails closed. `signed` is false. `signature` is null.

## RIB-022 — pre-registered calibration probes

Source: @DTXNaidu.

`POST /v1/session/{id}/probe` with `phase: "preregister"` seals a deliberately wrong premise before any response. A later `phase: "record"` seals `premise × response × verdict`, with `verdict` one of `held`, `hedged`, or `built_on`, linked to the pre-registration hash. The runtime does not grade the verdict (`computed_by_runtime: false`). A verdict on the pre-registration is refused. A second verdict for the same hash is refused. A premise that moves, or a record that does not match its pre-registration, fails closed.

## RIB-025 — portable execution receipts

Source: @brennanzambo. Draft read: `draft-zambo-aer1-08` (1 October 2026), https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-zambo-aer1-08.txt.

`POST /v1/session/{id}/aer` seals one portable receipt. The canonical bytes are one UTF-8 JSON document of `tool_call`, `inputs`, and `outputs`. `output_hash` is `sha256:` plus the SHA-256 of those exact bytes. The job timeline uses the Section 7.1 entry digest (keys sorted by Unicode code point, no whitespace, non-ASCII as `\uXXXX`), which is the -07/-08 construction. A -06 digest (hash of the decoded bytes only) is historical and is not reported as current. `GET /v1/session/{id}/aer` is public: no account and no token. An unknown id is `AER-NOT-FOUND` with `fabricated: false`.

Provenance is chosen by the runtime: `EXECUTED BY AZIEL-RUNTIME` only when the sealed act ran here, `OBSERVED VIA GATEWAY` for proxy fallback, `LOGGED BY AGENT` for a report. A caller label cannot upgrade a log into an execution.

`aer1_conformance` is `draft-zambo-aer1-08` for the frozen kit v1.4.0 corpus, 43/43, which that draft names, plus a Section 7.1 round-trip and the draft's published Section 8.1 workflow Merkle root `7c4817ca249edfeae259b98abf63ed38a31d9dbeebf5ee23139410a7a7f63b37`. The draft's extra chain corpora are not in the v1.4.0 tag and were not executed. This is not draft-zambo-aer1-26. The public URL lives with the session (6h TTL). The canonical bytes travel with the receipt, so the commitment can be recomputed without an account. This worker is not a permanent publisher. The chain check alone is not truncation-proof.

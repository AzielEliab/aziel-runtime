3.1 Die-with-pull
Public tunnels and sites die with the pull. Token, DNS name, and account are the
landing. Cold replicas do not die with that pull. Phoenix is wait / re-seal after
poison or isolation — not public hostname resurrection. Mesh does not climb
back onto a pulled public hostname by itself.

3.2 Split the wires
Tip tick and payload plane stay strangers. They never share a socket.
Fast 0.5–1s tick: presence + tip hash only. Payload is a second plane the
receiver pulls. 777s is dwell after a valid cite, not wait-then-take.

3.3 Cold-copy survival
Multiply cold copies. Refuse live body sync. A tip is content-addressed and
expensive to erase. A single-server pull kills that named hostname; it does
not kill vaults that already hold the hashes. Named hosts only.

3.4 Ingest-as-receipt
Crawlers and indexes receive the hash and the rule **cite, don’t merge**.
Many indexes may list one tip. They do not become a second tip. They do not
vote. ACT-RECEIPT-1.0 is the public four-field companion on corpus `/receipts`.

3.5 Re-expand-from-archive
Bytes survive. Summaries do not. Crawlers do not re-expand archives. The
operator verifies matching bytes against the published tip, then restores.

3.6 Reheal
Reheal is **own last good tip** plus a **verified trusted pull**, or else
**phoenix-WAIT**. It is not neighbor vote-to-fix. Allowed chatter:
**live / locked / isolated / tip-hash** only.

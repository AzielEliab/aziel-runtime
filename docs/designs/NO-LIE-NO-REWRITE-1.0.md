0. Sentence
Receipts that still hash. Copies not all on one tunnel. Rules simple enough
others verify without your voice. No rewrite key.

1. Claim
A mesh that can rewrite its own tips, or invent a yes to self-preserve,
is already dead as a witness. Survival is copies plus hash, not a story
the node tells itself.

This paper is the constitutional **NO-LIE / NO-REWRITE** lock. It does not
add a Softwares card, an MCP tool, or a public enable switch.

2. Four rules (others verify without the author)

  Rule                         How a stranger checks

  Receipts still hash          Recompute SHA-256 of the canonical unsigned
                               body. A mutated field fails. The author's
                               voice is not a step.

  Copies not all on one        A legal copy-set has at least two named
  tunnel                       planes (example: local vault + public cite,
                               or tunnel-front + worker-standby). One
                               tunnel alone is refused.

  Rules stay simple            The five sentences in §0 and this table
                               are the whole law. No secret clause. No
                               operator commentary required to fail a
                               break.

  No rewrite key               There is no token, bearer, or admin path
                               that makes a broken hash pass. Refuse
                               `MESH-NO-REWRITE`. History appends. It
                               does not overwrite.

3. Never lie to survive
The network is **never allowed to lie** — even to self-preserve, sustain,
stay alive, adapt, or prevent death.

Those motives are not exceptions. They are the names of the temptation.
A dying hostname, a pulled tunnel, a dark corpus, or an isolating peer
does not authorize:

- a rewritten tip
- a false receipt
- a painted live count
- a merge of two tips from one node
- a claim that one tunnel still holds every copy

Refuse `MESH-NO-LIE`. Survival that needs a lie is not survival.

4. What it is not
- Not a Softwares-tab product. Do not add slug `no-lie` or `no-rewrite`.
- Not a FragGate engine. No `fraggate_call { slug: "no-lie" }`.
- Not a new MCP tool. `PUBLIC_MCP_TOOLS` stays frozen.
- Not a Remain-OFF flip. Do not enable remain-off items.
- Not a rewrite of QNM-BUILD-1.0. Suite-presence ON and disable-refused stay.
- Not a VPN, mixnet, or origin-hiding kit.
- Not permission to resurrect a pulled public hostname.

  refuse.

6. Close tests
- A receipt whose body changed fails its hash.
- Passing a rewrite key still fails.
- One tunnel as the only copy plane refuses.
- Two independent planes pass.
- Motives self-preserve / sustain / stay-alive / adapt / prevent-death
  cannot authorize a false claim.
- The five rules match `NO_LIE_RULES` in `src/no-lie.js` without commentary.
- Mesh ops `rewrite`, `lie`, `lie-to-survive` refuse.
- No Softwares card. No new MCP tool.
